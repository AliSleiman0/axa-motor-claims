Three real steps, in this order: the browser draws the decision and comments as an image, uploads it, and only then commits the approval. Naming them matters because a failure at step two must leave the declaration undecided — and the officer needs to know which step they are on.

Both buttons disable together; they share one latch, so a second press cannot start a second decision.
