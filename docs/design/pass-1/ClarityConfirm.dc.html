Every refusal names what to do differently, not what the code decided. The resolution refusal is its sibling: use the rear camera at full quality rather than a screenshot or a forwarded copy.

Caption rule: the 1600×1200, sharpness 312 line appears only where there is something measured — a photograph. Audio, PDFs and the rendered damage diagram carry no caption at all, because a number invented for them would be a number somebody trusts.
