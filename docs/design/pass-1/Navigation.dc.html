Rules the shells follow

Two destinations, never three. There is no third screen a field user needs while standing at a car, and a two-item bar keeps both targets far from the thumb's edge.
The bar sits above the home indicator: 64 px of bar plus the device safe area, drawn here as the extra 20 px.
No fake status bar and no painted keyboard anywhere in this design — the real ones render on top.
Back is an in-page link, not a nav item: ← My claims at the top of a detail screen, carrying any active search back with it.
Net-new: both the phone bar and Sign out are new surface. The build today has navigation only on the admin screens.
